PLANT INFORMATION

1) निम्ब (Nimba)

1. Nimba Taila

→ Main Effect: Skin-supporting, antimicrobial

→ Neem Part: Neem seed oil

→ Other Ingredients: Sesame oil

→ Why Neem is Used: Traditional antimicrobial and skin-supporting properties.

2. Nimba Ghrita

→ Main Effect: Skin-supporting

→ Neem Part: Neem

→ Other Ingredients: Go-ghrita

→ Why Neem is Used: Traditional use in skin-related Ayurvedic preparations.

3. Nimbadi Kwatha

→ Main Effect: Traditional support for skin and fever-related conditions

→ Neem Part: Neem

→ Other Ingredients: Formulation-specific herbs

→ Why Neem is Used: Neem is traditionally included for its bitter and skin-supporting properties.

4. Panchatikta Ghrita

→ Main Effect: Traditional support for skin and inflammatory conditions

→ Neem Part: Stem and bark

→ Other Ingredients: Patola, Guduchi, Vasa, Ghrita

→ Why Neem is Used: Neem is one of the principal bitter herbs in the Panchatikta group.

5. Nimbamritadi Taila

→ Main Effect: Traditional skin-supporting use

→ Neem Part: Neem

→ Other Ingredients: Guduchi, Patola, oil base

→ Why Neem is Used: Neem is included as a key herb in this traditional medicated-oil formulation.

2) हरिद्रा (Haridra)

1. Haridra Khanda

→ Main Effect: Traditional support for skin and allergy-related conditions

→ Turmeric Part: Haridra rhizome

→ Other Ingredients: Formulation-specific herbs and spices

→ Why Turmeric is Used: Haridra is traditionally valued for skin-supporting properties.

2. Haridra Churna

→ Main Effect: Traditional digestive and skin-supporting use

→ Turmeric Part: Haridra rhizome

→ Other Ingredients: Depends on the preparation

→ Why Turmeric is Used: The rhizome is the principal medicinal part of Haridra.

3. Nisha Amalaki

→ Main Effect: Traditional support for metabolic health

→ Turmeric Part: Haridra rhizome

→ Other Ingredients: Amalaki

→ Why Turmeric is Used: Haridra is traditionally combined with Amalaki in this preparation.

4. Haridra Taila

→ Main Effect: Traditional skin-supporting use

→ Turmeric Part: Haridra rhizome

→ Other Ingredients: Oil base and formulation-specific ingredients

→ Why Turmeric is Used: Traditionally used in external preparations for skin care.

5. Haridra-based Preparations

→ Main Effect: Traditional digestive and skin-supporting use

→ Turmeric Part: Haridra rhizome

→ Other Ingredients: Depends on the formulation

→ Why Turmeric is Used: Haridra is an important traditional Ayurvedic herb.

3) गुडूची (Guduchi)

1. Guduchi Satva

→ Main Effect: Traditional support for general wellness and fever-related conditions

→ Giloy Part: Giloy stem

→ Other Ingredients: Prepared primarily from Guduchi

→ Why Giloy is Used: Guduchi is traditionally valued for its bitter and supportive properties.

2. Guduchyadi Churna

→ Main Effect: Traditional digestive and wellness support

→ Giloy Part: Giloy stem

→ Other Ingredients: Formulation-specific herbs

→ Why Giloy is Used: Guduchi is traditionally included as an important herb in the formulation.

3. Amritarishta

→ Main Effect: Traditional support for fever-related conditions

→ Giloy Part: Giloy stem

→ Other Ingredients: Dashamoola, Trikatu and other formulation-specific ingredients

→ Why Giloy is Used: Guduchi is traditionally used as an important ingredient in this preparation.

4. Guduchi Ghrita

→ Main Effect: Traditional general wellness support

→ Giloy Part: Giloy stem

→ Other Ingredients: Ghrita and formulation-specific ingredients

→ Why Giloy is Used: Guduchi is traditionally used in ghrita-based Ayurvedic preparations.

5. Guduchi Taila

→ Main Effect: Traditional external-use preparation

→ Giloy Part: Giloy stem

→ Other Ingredients: Oil base and formulation-specific ingredients

→ Why Giloy is Used: Guduchi is traditionally included in selected medicated-oil preparations.
